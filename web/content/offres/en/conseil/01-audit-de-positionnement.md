BIG FIVE carries out positioning audits for brands and organizations that want to know whether their image matches their business reality, in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire (Ivory Coast), and across French-speaking Africa. The audit compares what you say about yourself, what your audiences perceive and what your competitors do. It gives you a factual basis before any decision on strategy, communications or digital investment.

## What the audit covers

We look at your brand from several angles. The goal is not to grade your communications. It is to measure the gap between the image you project and the image people actually receive.

- Your brand promise, your key messages and how consistent they are.
- Your digital presence: website, social media, content and conversations about the brand.
- How your audiences see you, based on social media listening and available feedback.
- How your direct competitors are positioned, and where there is still room in your market.

## How we work

The audit follows our firm's method: collect, decode, analyze, activate. First we gather the relevant information, drawing on the Monitoring & Intelligence team within our Intelligence & Consulting Division. Then we decode what the data says. Next we analyze the gaps against your objectives. The final step, activate, turns findings into concrete lines of action.

We work with your teams, not instead of them. Regular checkpoints let you validate findings as they emerge. As a digital marketing consultancy that grew out of hands-on work, we know a diagnosis is only useful if people inside the company understand it and own it.

## Who it is for

The audit is designed for CEOs, marketing directors and communications leads. It is most useful before a repositioning, a launch or an entry into a new French-speaking African market, or when results no longer reflect the effort invested. It also suits organizations that have never formalized their positioning and need a clear starting point.

## What you get

You receive a shared diagnosis, written to be discussed at leadership level. It sets out your strengths, the areas where your positioning is blurred and the gaps between image and reality. It ends with prioritized recommendations that can feed a marketing and digital strategy or an operational roadmap. With over 15 years of field experience, our consultants favor clear findings and realistic priorities over long reports.
