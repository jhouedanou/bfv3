BIG FIVE designs marketing and digital strategies for brands and organizations that want clear choices grounded in their real market, in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire (Ivory Coast), and across French-speaking Africa. The strategy sets your objectives, your target audiences, your messages and the role of each channel. It connects your marketing to business results, instead of piling up actions with no common thread.

## What the strategy covers

A digital strategy for Côte d'Ivoire cannot be copied from an imported template. Usage patterns, channels and audience expectations have their own realities. So we build the strategy from your context.

- Marketing objectives tied to your business objectives.
- Priority audiences and what drives them.
- Positioning and key messages.
- The role of each channel, online and offline, along the customer journey.
- Performance indicators to track what works.

## How we work

We apply our firm's method: collect, decode, analyze, activate. The strategy rests on what is known about your market, not on gut feeling. Our Monitoring & Intelligence team continuously tracks how usage and algorithms evolve. The BIG FIVE Observatory publishes a sector barometer every week. This work feeds directly into our recommendations.

The engagement is led by the Intelligence & Consulting Division, headed by Executive Director Yannick Boka. Your teams are involved at every stage: scoping workshops, validation of key directions, trade-offs. A strategy your people helped build is a strategy they can defend and apply.

## Who it is for

This offer is for CEOs and marketing directors who want to bring coherence back to their actions, prepare a budget cycle, launch a brand or product, or structure a scattered digital presence. It suits companies and institutions alike that are looking for digital marketing consulting rooted in field reality.

## What you get

You get a clear strategy document, well-argued directions and performance indicators (KPIs) to measure progress. The strategy then becomes a prioritized action plan, which we can detail further in an operational roadmap. Our conviction, built over more than 15 years of field experience: your teams don't need executors, they need a partner who makes them autonomous.
