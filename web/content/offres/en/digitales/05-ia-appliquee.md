BIG FIVE brings artificial intelligence into the tools of companies and organisations in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire (Ivory Coast), and across French-speaking Africa, for specific uses you can measure. We do not sell AI as a broad promise. We start from a real task that is time-consuming or repetitive, and we check whether AI can make it simpler, faster or more reliable. That is what our promise means: technology, proven.

## What we build

Applied AI is most useful on well-defined tasks, with human review where it matters. Our work includes:

- Automating repetitive tasks: sorting, classifying and extracting information from documents
- Analysing text and conversations: comments, reviews, customer messages and field reports
- Internal assistants that help your teams find information in your own documents
- Drafting and summarising support, always reviewed by your teams
- Building these features into your existing tools: CRM, apps and platforms

## How we work

We first identify, with you, the tasks where AI can genuinely help. We then run a limited trial on your own data, so the quality of the results can be judged on evidence. If the trial is conclusive, we build the feature into your tools, with clear rules on what the AI does on its own and what a person still approves.

Data protection is central to every project. We define which data can be processed, by which service and under which conditions. We restrict access, avoid exposing sensitive information and choose tools according to the level of confidentiality required. Your data stays yours.

## Who it is for

This offer is for leadership teams that want to use AI for business in Côte d'Ivoire without getting lost in the hype, for marketing and customer service teams handling large volumes of messages, and for organisations that want to make better use of their internal documents. It follows on naturally from our monitoring and social media listening work.

## What you get

You get a solution that is deployed and maintained, data you can act on, and dashboards showing what the AI processes and how well. Your teams stay in control and know what the tool does, and what it does not.
