BIG FIVE designs SaaS platforms and white-label solutions for companies and organisations that want to offer a service online, in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire (Ivory Coast), and across French-speaking Africa. A SaaS platform is software people access through a browser, usually on a subscription. A white-label solution is a tool you distribute under your own name to your customers or your network. In both cases, we build the platform, host it and keep it evolving.

## What we build

We know this kind of project from the inside. Laveiye, the library of advertising campaigns in Africa, is a platform designed and powered by BIG FIVE. We bring the same approach to our clients’ platforms:

- Multi-tenant SaaS platforms with separate accounts, roles and workspaces
- White-label solutions that each partner can brand as their own
- Admin areas to manage customers, content and access
- Usage and activity dashboards
- Connections to your existing tools and data sources

## How we work

A platform project starts with one question: who pays, for which service, and how do we measure it? We work with you to clarify the model, the user profiles and the features you need at launch. We then design a focused first version that real users can try early.

We pay close attention to what keeps a platform standing over time: data separation between customers, permissions, access security, backups and the ability to welcome new users. The platform then grows release by release, guided by your feedback.

## Who it is for

This offer suits companies that want to turn their know-how into an online service, groups and networks that want to equip subsidiaries or partners with a shared tool, and organisations that need a custom SaaS platform in Côte d'Ivoire rather than generic software. It also fits businesses that want to resell a white-label solution under their own name.

## What you get

You get a solution that is deployed and maintained, usable data on how the platform is used, and dashboards to steer it. We remain responsible for hosting, maintenance and new features, so you can focus on your customers.
