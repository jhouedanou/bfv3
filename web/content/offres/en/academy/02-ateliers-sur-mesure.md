BIG FIVE designs tailored workshops for the marketing and communications teams of companies and organizations in Abidjan and across French-speaking Africa. Each workshop starts from a real question your team is facing and ends with a concrete, usable result. It is one of the formats of Big Five Academy, our capacity-building and skills-transfer offering.

## What a workshop covers

A workshop is not a general course. It tackles one specific topic, chosen with you. Possible themes include:

- Building a brand's editorial line and content calendar.
- Preparing a product or campaign launch on digital channels.
- Organizing community management and handling sensitive comments.
- Reading monitoring data and turning it into better decisions.
- Aligning marketing, communications and sales teams around shared goals.

## How we work

It starts with a conversation to understand your context, your goals and your team's level. We then design the workshop around your own material: your brands, your audiences, your constraints. During the session, your team does the work, with us alongside. We come from hands-on operational work, and we run these sessions as practitioners with over 15 years of field experience.

To show what works in the market, the firm's own work can feed the discussion: the Observatory's sector barometers, or campaigns cataloged in Laveiye, the library of advertising campaigns in Africa.

## Who it is for

Marketing, communications and digital teams facing a specific challenge and wanting to move quickly. Managers looking for in-house, custom training in Côte d'Ivoire (Ivory Coast) rather than an off-the-shelf program. Organizations that already have the basics and want to go further on one topic. A workshop can bring together a single team or several departments that need to work together.

## What you get

At the end of a workshop, your team leaves with a deliverable it built itself and knows how to update. It also leaves with reusable materials and tools so it can repeat the process without us. A learning assessment confirms what has been mastered. That is what our conviction means in practice: your teams don't need hired hands, but a partner who makes them autonomous and high-performing.
