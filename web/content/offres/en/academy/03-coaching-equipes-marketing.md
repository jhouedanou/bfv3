BIG FIVE offers marketing team coaching in Abidjan and across French-speaking Africa for companies that want their teams to build skills on their own projects, over time. Training passes on knowledge; coaching helps teams put it into practice, at the pace of the business. It is one of the formats of Big Five Academy, the firm's skills-transfer offer.

## What coaching involves

The coach works with your team on live files: a campaign to prepare, a content strategy to rethink, results to analyze, an organization to structure. The coach asks the right questions, brings methods, reviews the work and helps the team decide. The coach does not do the work for them. Digital marketing coaching can focus on:

- Digital strategy and action planning.
- Steering community management and content.
- Reading the indicators and adjusting campaigns.
- Team organization, roles and approval processes.

## How we work

We start with a shared diagnosis: where the team stands, what it already masters, what holds it back. We agree on progress goals together, then move forward through regular sessions tied to real work. We were born in operations, and we coach with over 15 years of field experience in French-speaking African markets. The firm's monitoring work, such as the Observatoire or Laveiye, the library of advertising campaigns in Africa, can feed the sessions with market benchmarks.

## Who it is for

Marketing directors who have built or renewed a team and want it up to speed. Managers stepping into a new role who want an outside perspective. Companies bringing digital marketing in-house after working with an agency. Any coaching engagement in Côte d'Ivoire (Ivory Coast) or elsewhere in the region begins with a conversation to check that coaching is the right format.

## What you get

A team that decides and delivers on its own, using methods it has made its own by applying them. Reusable materials and tools, built during the engagement. Learning assessments along the way to measure progress. Our conviction guides the work: your teams don't need executors, but a partner who makes them autonomous and high-performing. Coaching succeeds when you no longer need us.
