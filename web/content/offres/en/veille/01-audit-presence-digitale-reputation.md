BIG FIVE conducts digital presence and reputation audits for brands, companies and institutions operating in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire (Ivory Coast), and across French-speaking Africa. The audit gives you a factual picture of your brand online: where it shows up, how it speaks, what people say about it and how it compares with competitors.

## What the audit covers

We review every digital touchpoint of your brand. The goal is not to produce an inventory, but to understand what those touchpoints say about you.

- Your owned channels: website, social media accounts, published content, consistency of messages.
- Your visibility: how you appear in search results and in online conversations.
- Your online reputation: reviews, comments, articles and mentions that shape how the brand is perceived.
- Your relative position: what other players in your sector do on the same channels.

## Our method

The audit follows the first two steps of our Collect · Decode · Analyze · Activate method. We first collect public data about your brand and your market. We then decode it to separate noise from useful signals: recurring topics, repeated criticism, and the strengths your audience already recognizes.

The work is led by our Intelligence & Consulting Division, under Cossi, Research & Monitoring Lead. Our reading draws on over 15 years of field experience and on continuous tracking of how African internet users behave online. A reputation audit in Côte d'Ivoire cannot be read like one carried out elsewhere: platforms, codes and opinion leaders are different.

## Who it is for

The audit is designed for marketing and communications teams that need a solid starting point. It is useful before a website redesign, a launch, a repositioning or a change of agency. It also helps when a crisis or a wave of online criticism calls for a clear view of how the brand is really perceived.

## What you get

You receive an analysis report setting out our findings, the gaps between the image you intend and the image people perceive, and prioritized recommendations. We separate quick fixes from longer-term work. The audit can then serve as the baseline for ongoing monitoring or for a strategic consulting engagement.
