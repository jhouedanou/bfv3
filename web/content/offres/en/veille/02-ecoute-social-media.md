BIG FIVE provides social media listening for brands that want to understand online conversations in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire (Ivory Coast), and across French-speaking Africa. We track what people say about your brand, your products, your competitors and your sector, then turn it into insights your teams can use.

## What social listening covers

Social listening is more than counting mentions. It means understanding what conversations actually say: who is talking, about what, in what tone, and what that means for your brand.

- Mentions of your brand, products and campaigns.
- The tone of conversations and how it shifts over time.
- Recurring topics, pain points and expectations in your audience.
- The voices that matter in your sector and the communities that amplify messages.
- Conversations about your competitors and your category.

## Our method

We apply the Collect and Decode steps of our method. First, we agree with you on the topics to follow, the relevant platforms and the countries to cover. We then collect the matching public conversations. Decoding is the core of the work: we qualify what is said, filter out the noise and highlight what deserves your attention.

Listening is run by the team of Cossi, Research & Monitoring Lead, within our Intelligence & Consulting Division. We continuously track how algorithms and African internet users' habits evolve. That field knowledge matters: in Côte d'Ivoire and elsewhere in French-speaking Africa, language, humor and local references change how a message should be read.

## Who it is for

The service is designed for marketing, communications and customer relations teams. Use it to gauge how a campaign or launch is received, to spot early a complaint that is gaining traction, or to learn what your customers really expect. It also helps institutions that need to follow public opinion on a given issue.

## What you get

Depending on your needs, listening can be a one-off around a specific event or an ongoing setup. You get a dashboard, periodic analysis reports and alerts when the situation calls for them. Every report ends with concrete recommendations, so listening feeds your decisions and protects your online reputation.
