BIG FIVE runs online training programs, the E-Masterclass, for marketing and communications teams in Abidjan and across French-speaking Africa. They are designed for organizations whose teams are spread across several cities or countries, or that cannot bring everyone together in one place. The E-Masterclass is part of Big Five Academy, the firm's capacity-building offering.

## What the E-Masterclass covers

An E-Masterclass covers the same ground as our social media and digital marketing training, in a format built for remote learning. Depending on your needs, an online digital marketing course can focus on:

- Digital strategy and setting objectives.
- Community management and editorial line.
- Content creation for social networks.
- Online advertising and reading the results.
- Performance tracking and dashboards.

## How we work

Learning online should not mean learning alone in front of a screen. Our programs are built by practitioners who come from hands-on operational work and bring over 15 years of field experience. Every concept is tied to a real case and to an exercise participants apply to their own work. Examples come from African markets: the Observatory's sector barometers and Laveiye, the library of advertising campaigns in Africa, can feed the content with real cases from the region.

Format, pace and level of support are agreed with you. An E-Masterclass can also be combined with a workshop or coaching to make the learning stick.

## Who it is for

Companies operating in several French-speaking African countries that want a common foundation for all their teams. Organizations in Côte d'Ivoire (Ivory Coast) that want to train staff outside Abidjan without having them travel. Marketing and communications professionals who want to learn at their own pace, alongside their day-to-day work.

## What you get

Teams that share the same methods, wherever they are based. Reusable materials and tools they can return to after the program. A learning assessment to confirm what has been mastered and spot what needs more work. True to our conviction, we are not trying to create dependency: your teams don't need hired hands, but a partner who makes them autonomous and high-performing.
